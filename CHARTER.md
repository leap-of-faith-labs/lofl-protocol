Licence: CC-BY-SA-4.0 · Steward: Leap of Faith Labs (OPC) Private Limited, until §5 fires.

## 1. Three layers, three rules

| Layer | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Protocol** | the promise, evidence, deliberation and envelope specs and schemas in this repository | Open now under CC-BY-SA-4.0. Anyone builds on it without asking. |
| **Engines** | the software that runs the protocol — the cards forge, the kit forge, the Karandak engine, the connectors | Private until the gate in §2 is met by any organisation. Then open to everyone under CC-BY-SA-4.0. |
| **Service** | what Leap of Faith Labs runs: hosting records, checking signatures, funder views, discovery | Operated by LOFL and paid for. Every record in it can always be exported as protocol files. |

## 2. The gate

An organisation meets the gate when **both** of these are in the public record:

- **(a) a completed sprint** — a `sprint` record with `status: completed`, issued as a self-signed promise by the organisation; and
- **(b) one L1 record** — an evidence record whose verification is a promise, self-signed by a witness who is not the organisation, naming the evidence by its fingerprint.

Both are published under CC-BY-SA-4.0. LOFL checks them with a script in this repository that anyone can run. No interview, no judgement, no LOFL signature.

## 3. What happens when the gate is met

LOFL issues its own promise: *"I will release the engines, at a named version, under CC-BY-SA-4.0, to [organisation], within 14 days."* Then it does.

**Release to one is release to all.** Once any organisation holds the engines under CC-BY-SA-4.0, it may share them with anyone. The gate buys LOFL a head start, not control. We say so here so no one buys a lock we do not have.

## 5. The trigger

The protocol, this charter, and the engines once released, pass to an **independent commons body with at least two stewards who are not LOFL's operator**, at the earlier of:

- the first external contributor's change being merged into this repository; or
- the first raise for LOFL closing. **[M: confirm both]**

Until then, Leap of Faith Labs (OPC) Private Limited is the steward, and says so.

## 6. What never changes, in any layer

- No score, rank or rating anywhere.
- No personal data about the people being served.
- Promises are about one's own behaviour only.
- Nothing is deleted; records are superseded.
- No art generated by AI.
- Prices are fixed on the day of sale and never rise or fall with outcomes.
- LOFL's builder is its founder (mrnm-ai). That relationship, its rates and every payment are published.
